What assistance includes

What you actually get
from us.

Our role is practical rather than promotional. In plain terms, here is what we do on a file.

  • An honest read of where your profile is likely to stand
  • An explanation of which products suit the requirement, and which do not
  • A precise document checklist, and a review of every document before submission
  • Submission of the application to the lending institutions you authorise
  • Follow-up through underwriting, and help answering the lender's queries
  • A clear explanation of the sanction terms before you sign anything

What we do not do

Being clear about this saves everybody time.

  • We do not lend money — we are not a bank or an NBFC
  • We do not approve loans or decide your interest rate
  • We do not promise approval, and we will not pretend to
  • We do not ask for advance cash, OTPs, passwords or card PINs
