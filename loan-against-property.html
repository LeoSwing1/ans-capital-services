Cheap money, with real consequences

You mortgage a property you own — residential, commercial or industrial — and borrow against its value. The lender holds the title documents until the loan closes. Because the security is strong, rates run from about 8.60% to 15% per annum, against 14% to 24% for an unsecured business loan. On a ₹50 lakh facility, that difference is several lakh rupees a year.

Funding is conservative by design. Expect 70% to 75% of the lender's assessed market value for residential property, and 50% to 65% for commercial or industrial. The lender's valuation is its own, and it is frequently below what you believe the property is worth — build that into your planning rather than being surprised by it.

The honest warning belongs here, not in the fine print. This is not a personal loan with a better rate. If repayment fails, the lender can proceed against the property under the SARFAESI Act, and the process is faster than most borrowers expect. Take a LAP for a productive purpose with a clear repayment source — business expansion, consolidating expensive debt, a child's education abroad. Do not take one to fund consumption.

A loan against property is the cheapest money a business owner can raise, and the only one where the downside is the roof over your family. Both halves of that sentence are true at once.

What people use it for

  • Business expansion, a new unit or large working capital
  • Consolidating expensive unsecured loans and card debt into one cheap EMI
  • Funding higher education in India or abroad
  • A major medical event where the sum is large and the timeline is long
  • Buying another property, or funding its down payment
  • Settling a family or partnership division where cash is required

Types of Loan Against Property we arrange

The purpose and the structure change both the pricing and how much you actually end up paying in interest. An overdraft structure alone can save a business lakhs.

LAP — term loan

The standard structure. Full amount disbursed, fixed EMI, long tenure. Right for a one-time, defined requirement.

Dropline overdraft against property

A limit against your property where the sanctioned limit reduces annually and interest applies only on what you draw. Far cheaper for fluctuating business needs.

Loan against commercial property

Shops, offices, godowns and industrial sheds. Lower funding percentage, slightly higher rate, larger absolute ticket.

Loan against rent receivables

Borrow against a registered lease on your commercial property. Underwritten on the tenant's covenant and the lease term.

LAP balance transfer & top-up

Move an expensive existing LAP to a cheaper lender and draw additional funds on the enhanced valuation.

Lease rental discounting

For large commercial assets with corporate tenants — the rental stream itself is discounted into a lump sum today.

Who qualifies

Two files are assessed in parallel — yours and the property's. Most LAP rejections are caused by the property, not the borrower.

Applicant criteria

  • Age 21 to 70 years at loan maturity
  • Salaried or self-employed with documented, provable income
  • Self-employed: 3+ years of business with filed ITR
  • CIBIL 700+ preferred; secured lending allows some flexibility
  • Total EMIs within 55–65% of net monthly income
  • Clear ownership or co-ownership of the property offered

Property criteria

  • Clear, marketable title with the full ownership chain
  • Freehold preferred; leasehold accepted with the lessor's NOC
  • Construction approved and matching the sanctioned plan
  • Property within the municipal limits the lender lends in
  • Free of litigation, encroachment and existing mortgage
  • Up-to-date property tax receipts and an encumbrance certificate

Documents you'll need

Self-employed / business

  • Last 12 months' current-account statement
  • GST registration and last 6–12 months' GST returns
  • Business proof — Udyam, Shop Act, partnership deed or MOA/AOA

Property documents

  • Complete title chain — sale deed and all parent documents
  • Approved building plan and completion or occupancy certificate
  • Latest property tax receipt and electricity bill
  • Encumbrance certificate for the last 13 to 30 years
  • Possession or allotment letter, where applicable
  • Share certificate and society NOC for flats
  • Lease deed and tenant details, if the property is rented

How to get a better deal

Get your own valuation first

The lender's valuation sets your loan amount, and it is often 10% to 20% below market expectation. Knowing the realistic number before you apply prevents you from planning around a figure that will not arrive.

Clear the title before you apply, not during

An old unreleased mortgage, a missing parent document, a name mismatch across deeds — each adds weeks. Sorting these out before submission is the single biggest determinant of how fast a LAP completes.

Ask for the dropline overdraft structure

If the money is for business, a dropline OD charges interest only on what you actually use. Businesses routinely take a term loan, park the surplus in a current account, and pay interest on money sitting idle.

Read the foreclosure clause with care

Prepayment protection for individual floating-rate borrowers does not extend to company borrowers or business-purpose loans. On a twenty-year facility you may well want to exit in year six. Know the cost before you sign.
