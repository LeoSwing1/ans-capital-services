Five more products worth knowing about

Most borrowers arrive asking for one specific product and leave having found that something adjacent suits them better. A parent asking for a personal loan to pay tuition often qualifies for an education loan at half the rate, with repayment that only starts after the course ends. A business owner servicing three expensive unsecured loans frequently does better consolidating them than adding a fourth.

This page covers the rest of the shelf. Each of these is a real product with real conditions, and for each of them there is a situation in which it is the wrong answer. We will say so.

The useful service is not access to more products. It is someone willing to tell you which of them you should not take.

Types of Education, Gold, Cards & Insurance we arrange

Here is each one in more detail, including the part that usually gets skipped over at the counter.

Education loan — India & abroad

Covers tuition, living costs, travel and equipment. Repayment begins after the course plus a grace period. Collateral-free up to ₹7.5 lakh under CGFSEL; larger amounts need security or a strong co-applicant. Interest paid qualifies for deduction under Section 80E, with no upper limit.

Gold loan

The fastest cash in Indian retail finance — valuation and disbursal in a couple of hours. RBI permits lending up to 75% of gold value. Short tenures of six to twenty-four months. Repay on time: this is the one product where the asset can genuinely be auctioned, and quickly.

Credit cards

Useful for cash-flow timing and rewards, ruinous if you revolve a balance at 36% to 45% a year. We match the card to how you actually spend — fuel, groceries, travel — rather than to whatever has the largest signup bonus.

Insurance

Term life at a young age costs very little and is the single highest-value financial product most families never buy. Health cover must be in place before illness, not after. We distribute from IRDAI-registered insurers and will explain the exclusions.

Balance transfer & top-up

If your existing loan is priced above today's market, moving it can cut your EMI materially, and a top-up on a home loan costs far less than a fresh personal loan. We will also tell you when the transfer cost outweighs the saving.

Working capital & invoice discounting

Short-term facilities for businesses waiting on receivables — bill discounting, cash credit and supply-chain finance through network lenders.

Who qualifies

Each product has its own test. These are the broad ones most applicants need to clear.

Education loan

  • Indian citizen with a confirmed admission
  • Recognised course at a recognised institution
  • Co-applicant — parent, guardian or spouse — with documented income
  • Collateral for loans above ₹7.5 lakh
  • Academic record consistent with the course applied for
  • Co-applicant CIBIL 700+ preferred

Gold loan, cards & insurance

  • Gold loan: age 18+, gold of 18 to 22 carat purity, owned by the applicant
  • Credit card: documented income from roughly ₹20,000 a month, CIBIL 700+
  • Insurance: age and medical underwriting as per the insurer's policy
  • Balance transfer: 12+ months of clean repayment on the existing loan
  • Top-up: existing home loan or LAP with a good track record

How to get a better deal

Apply for an education loan before you apply for a personal loan

Education finance is priced at roughly half a personal loan, repayment is deferred until after the course, and the interest is deductible under Section 80E with no upper limit. Parents routinely take personal loans for tuition without ever checking whether an education loan was available.

Treat a gold loan as short-term, genuinely

It is the quickest cash available and the easiest to lose an asset over. Tenures are short, renewal terms are strict and auction timelines are real. Borrow against gold for a bridge you can definitely close, never for an open-ended need.

Buy term life young and health cover early

A term policy taken at thirty costs a fraction of the same cover at forty-five, and health insurance bought after a diagnosis will exclude that condition. These are the two products where delay is the entire cost.

Check for a top-up before taking a personal loan

If you have a running home loan with a good record, a top-up is typically available at two to four percentage points below personal loan pricing, with a far longer tenure. It takes one phone call to find out.

Yes, up to ₹7.5 lakh under the Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Education Loans, where the government guarantee replaces security. Above that, banks generally want collateral — property, fixed deposits or securities — or a very strong co-applicant income. Some NBFCs and private lenders specialising in study-abroad finance will go higher without collateral for premier institutions and employable courses, at a higher rate.

After a moratorium that runs through the course plus six to twelve months, so repayment typically begins once the student starts earning. Interest accrues during the moratorium, and you have a choice: pay the simple interest monthly while studying, or let it capitalise into the principal. Paying it as you go is noticeably cheaper over the life of the loan, and many banks offer a small rate concession for doing so.

RBI permits lending up to 75% of the value of the gold. The value is calculated on the net weight of pure gold content — stones, and the making charges you originally paid, are excluded, which is why the valuation often comes in below what people expect. Purity between 18 and 22 carat is standard. Rates and the exact loan-to-value differ between banks and NBFCs, so it is worth comparing before you hand over the jewellery.
