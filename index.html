How it works

From enquiry
to decision.

Six steps. Most of it happens over the phone and WhatsApp, and you are told at each stage exactly what happens next.

Submit an enquiry

Share your requirement by phone, WhatsApp or the form on this website. It takes two minutes and commits you to nothing.

Discuss your requirements

We talk through the purpose, the amount, your income profile and any existing obligations, so the options we discuss are realistic.

Understand the available options

We explain which products suit your situation, what each typically costs and what the lender will look at. You decide what to pursue.

Complete the documentation

We give you a precise checklist and review every document with you before submission, so the file goes in complete.

Application review by the lender

The lending institution carries out its own verification, credit assessment and, where a property or vehicle is involved, valuation.

The lender communicates its decision

Approval, the amount, the rate and all conditions are decided solely by the lending institution and set out in its sanction letter.

Please note. Final eligibility, sanction and disbursement are determined solely by the relevant lending institution, under its own credit policy and verification process. ANS Capital Services assists with the application; it does not approve loans and cannot guarantee any outcome.

If a lender sanctions a loan, that lender levies its own processing and documentation charges, which are set out in your sanction letter and paid directly to the lender. If anyone ever asks you for an advance payment in our name, please stop and call us on +91 93196 67592 before paying anything.
