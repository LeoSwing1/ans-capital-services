Secured or unsecured — the decision that sets your rate

Business finance splits cleanly in two. An unsecured business loan is written against your financials and your banking behaviour, with no collateral. It is quick, usually three to ten working days, and priced at roughly 14% to 24% per annum because the lender holds nothing if the business stumbles.

A secured or MSME loan is backed by property, plant or a government guarantee under schemes such as CGTMSE. It takes longer to process, needs valuation and legal work, and prices from about 8.25% per annum. Across the market, unsecured business credit typically costs three to five percentage points more than a comparable secured facility — which, on a ₹50 lakh loan, is ₹1.5 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh a year in avoidable cost.

Structure matters as much as price. A term loan with a fixed EMI suits a machine purchase that will produce for seven years. It is the wrong instrument for seasonal stock, where an overdraft or cash-credit line — interest charged only on what you actually draw — is far cheaper in practice. We ask what the money is doing before we ask how much you need.

A term loan for working capital is the most common and most expensive mistake a growing business makes. Match the instrument to the cash cycle, not to whatever gets approved fastest.

What people use it for

  • Buying stock ahead of a season or a confirmed large order
  • Bridging receivables when customers pay on 60 or 90 day terms
  • Purchasing machinery, equipment or commercial vehicles
  • Fitting out a new branch, godown or outlet
  • Paying salaries, GST or statutory dues during a slow quarter
  • Consolidating expensive informal borrowing into formal, documented credit

Types of Business Loans we arrange

Six different structures sit under the phrase business loan. Choosing the wrong one is what makes business credit feel expensive.

Unsecured term loan

Fixed EMI, no collateral, assessed on ITR, GST returns and bank statements. Fast, and the default for amounts up to around ₹75 lakh.

Working capital / overdraft

A sanctioned limit you draw from as needed, with interest only on the utilised amount. The correct instrument for a seasonal or receivables-driven cycle.

Machinery & equipment loan

Funds the asset and is secured by it. Longer tenure, lower rate, and often eligible for subsidy under state MSME schemes.

Loan against property for business

The cheapest large-ticket business money available — your property backs a facility of ₹25 lakh to several crore at secured rates.

MSME & government schemes

Mudra, CGTMSE-backed collateral-free credit, Stand-Up India and state schemes. Paperwork-heavy, materially cheaper.

Invoice & bill discounting

Convert approved invoices into cash immediately rather than waiting out the credit period. Priced on the buyer's credit, not only yours.

Who qualifies

Underwriters read three things: how long the business has existed, what it actually earns on paper, and how the current account behaves. The third surprises people most.

Business criteria

  • Business vintage of 2 to 3 years (some NBFCs accept 1 year)
  • Annual turnover from ₹10 lakh for small-ticket, higher for larger facilities
  • Profitable in at least the last filed year, or improving trend
  • GST registration and regular, consistent filings
  • Udyam registration helps access MSME pricing
  • Current account with healthy credits and no cheque returns

Promoter criteria

  • Age 23 to 65 years at loan maturity
  • Personal CIBIL 700+ and commercial CIBIL / CMR of 6 or better
  • No default or settlement on existing facilities
  • Residence or office stability of 1+ year
  • Clear ownership structure with documented partners or directors

Documents you'll need

Send clear soft copies on WhatsApp and we will pre-check the whole set before anything goes to a lender. A file that is complete on day one is the single biggest reason a sanction comes back fast.

Business documents

  • PAN of the business entity
  • Udyam / GST registration certificate
  • Partnership deed or MOA, AOA and incorporation certificate
  • Shop & Establishment or trade licence
  • Last 2 years' ITR with computation
  • Audited P&L and balance sheet, last 2 years

Banking & tax

  • Last 12 months' current-account statement
  • Last 6 to 12 months' GST returns (GSTR-3B and GSTR-1)
  • Existing loan sanction letters and repayment track record
  • Debtors and creditors ageing statement
  • Latest provisional financials

Promoter KYC

  • PAN and Aadhaar of all partners or directors
  • Address proof — utility bill or rent agreement
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Ownership proof of the collateral, where the facility is secured

How to get a better deal

Fix the current account six months before you apply

Underwriters read your bank statement line by line. Cheque returns, a negative balance at month-end and cash deposits with no invoice trail all cost you rate — or the sanction. Six clean months of banking is the cheapest credit enhancement available to a small business.

File GST on time, every time

GST returns are now a primary income document for business lending. Consistent, timely filings that match your declared turnover open programmes that simply will not look at an irregular filer, whatever the profit looks like.

Use property if you have it

Three to five percentage points separate unsecured from secured credit. On ₹50 lakh that is ₹1.5 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh a year. If you own property and the requirement is long-term, a loan against property is nearly always the better instrument.

Separate the business and personal accounts

Mixed accounts make cash flow unreadable, and an underwriter who cannot read your cash flow prices for the worst case. A clean, separate current account genuinely moves your rate.

For seasonal stock or receivables gaps, an overdraft is usually far cheaper, because your money sits in the account rather than being borrowed and idle.
